## Full description (max 4000)
```
grail is a quiet place for writing that comes in pieces: thoughts, characters, places, half-formed ideas. Each note is a fragment. grail gathers them into one continuous read, so you can come back and read your own work like a book.

WRITE IN FRAGMENTS
• Tap + and start writing. A fragment keeps its date and time, with no title needed.
• Bold, italics, headings, lists and quotes, in plain markdown.
• Tables, edited as a simple grid.
• Pictures from your gallery, and drawings on a smooth pen canvas.

READ IT AS ONE STORY
• Read stitches your fragments into a single flowing text, set in a calm serif.
• Four layouts: Manuscript, Timeline, Codex and Cards.
• A contents drawer for chapters, loose notes and anything you've set aside.
• Swipe a fragment left to delete it, or right to lift it out and place it somewhere else in the story.

TAGS AS NOTEBOOKS
• Write #tags anywhere. Every tag becomes its own notebook with its own read.
• Search with queries like "#travel #2026" or "#idea -#done", and save the ones you use.

SNAPSHOTS
• Save a notebook as a Markdown note or a PDF, with its pictures, to keep a version or share it.

ECHOES
• Now and then, grail brings back an older fragment, especially on its anniversary.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Five themes: Ink, Reliquary, Vellum, Moss and Midnight.
• Serif, sans or mono reading text.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your notes stay on your phone, as plain markdown files.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Link previews, like in chat apps, only when you ask: grail goes online for nothing else.
```
